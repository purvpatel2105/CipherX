HOW IT WORKS

Secure messaging in four steps

No plugins, no installs. It all happens in your browser.

Create your account

Sign up and verify your email. Your encryption keys are generated on your device.

Share your ID

Copy your CipherX ID from the dashboard and give it to the people you want to hear from.

Write and send

Enter their ID and your message. It is encrypted before it ever leaves your browser.

They decrypt it

Only the recipient's private key can unlock the message, and only on their device.

SECURITY

Modern cryptography, no shortcuts

CipherX uses the standard, browser-native Web Crypto API. There is no home-made cryptography in the message path.

  • Private keys are locked with your password before they are stored
  • Your real password is never sent to the server
  • Each message uses its own random encryption key
  • Tampered messages fail to decrypt instead of showing garbage

Being transparent: the server can see who has an account and when messages are sent between IDs. It can never see what they say.
