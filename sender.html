PASSPHRASE ENCRYPTION

Encrypt & send a message

Encrypt text in your browser with a secret passphrase, store it, and share the Message ID. The receiver needs the ID and the passphrase to read it.

✅

Message stored securely

Share this Message ID with the receiver. Give them the passphrase through a different channel.

How it works

  1. Write your message and choose a passphrase.
  2. Your browser encrypts it. The passphrase stays on your device.
  3. The unreadable ciphertext is stored and you get a Message ID.
  4. The receiver enters the ID, then the passphrase, to decrypt.

Safety tips

  • Use 12+ characters, mixing words, numbers and symbols.
  • Never send the ID and passphrase in the same message.
  • If the passphrase is lost, the message cannot be recovered.
