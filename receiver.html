Integrity verified. The message was not altered in transit.

How it works

  1. Paste the Message ID and retrieve the ciphertext.
  2. The ciphertext is unreadable without the passphrase.
  3. Enter the passphrase. Your browser unlocks the message.

Decryption failed?

  • Check the passphrase for typos and capital letters.
  • Confirm the Message ID is complete, with no spaces.
  • A changed message will always fail. That is by design.
